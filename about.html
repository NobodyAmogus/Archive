About the archive

This is a personal collection, not a portfolio. Some of it is finished. Most of it isn't. It stays up anyway, because half-finished things are still worth being able to find again.

There's no editorial calendar here and no attempt to look busier than I am. Things get added when they exist, and they stay roughly as messy as they were when I made them.

If something here is useful to you, that's a nice coincidence. If it's a little strange or unfinished, that's on purpose.
